## Historical H1 panel rationale

A is a model/scale comparison, not a meta-analysis: within-cohort standardized scores, different covariates and donor counts are explicit. The unadjusted independent replication is retained to prevent “robust replication” wording from concealing adjustment dependence. B contains signed g, not a P-value ranking. Its missing frozen precision is declared, not replaced with made-up confidence limits. C includes all four modules rather than only the three dramatic primary effects; high VIF and same-cohort conditioning are visible.

Complete historical adjudication includes PBC, LN and HFpEF although they do not receive large H1 subpanels. Their omission from the illustration is a salience decision, not deletion of unfavorable cases. H1 cannot estimate the frequency of any failure mechanism.
