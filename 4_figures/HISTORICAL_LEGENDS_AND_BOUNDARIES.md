# Historical exhibits: separate development appendix, not R1 primary Results

H1 deliberately juxtaposes three distinct reasons a development story could not advance. It is a selected explanatory display, not a complete case-series analysis. The adjudication table in the audit folder includes all six disease-axis projects to disclose selection. No result, cohort, model, cutoff or NO_GO decision was changed; only previously frozen coefficients were plotted.

**H1A — Scale and model dependence in SSc IMPORT.** Points show coefficients from GSE130955 bulk discovery, GSE181549 independent baseline bulk replication (both unadjusted and adjusted), and GSE138669 donor-level fibroblast pseudobulk. Sample sizes are donors, not cells or repeated biopsies. Display bars are beta ± 1.96 times the frozen SE, a new graphical normal approximation rather than a new disease analysis or guaranteed exact small-sample interval. Module scores were standardized within each dataset; no pooled estimate or common cross-cohort magnitude is inferred. The independent baseline association depends on background-program adjustment. The fibroblast full model adjusts chemistry and log cell count, not the same high-dimensional covariate list as bulk. Negative nonsignificant pseudobulk does not establish biological absence. The failed localization gate limits the fibroblast-centered development claim.

**H1B — IgAN retrieval-complex direction reversal.** Hedges g is −1.5337 in discovery and +0.2908 in independent replication. Discovery uses an exact, four-module maxT two-sided family-wise test (P = 0.0064387); replication uses a frozen negative-direction one-sided Welch test (P = 0.8245092; two-sided P = 0.3509817). These P values do not constitute comparable effect sizes. The 13-gene frozen set had 12 measurable replication genes, with WASHC1 unavailable. Frozen summaries do not supply a CI or SE for g; no error bars are invented, and this panel is not a precision comparison.

**H1C — Endometriosis conditional background adjustment.** The context table's `unadjusted_beta` field is the phase-adjusted primary coefficient, not an entirely unadjusted contrast. Four modules are displayed without cherry-picking: NPC core, import, export and Ran. All points come from the same cohort; the conditional model is not independent replication. Primary-case/control sample sizes were 75/34. Maximum background-model VIF is 54.83, so coefficient attenuation can reflect collinearity and conditioning on disease-related pathways. It cannot by itself demonstrate causal confounding or absence of a transport phenotype. Context-model SEs are absent from the plotted table; the display is descriptive, with original primary CIs retained in the source report, not a complete inferential forest plot.

**H2 — Frozen synthetic classification failures.** This unchanged PNG/SVG displays V1 accuracy 146/300 and TRUE_ADVANCE recall 0/50, and V2 accuracy 265/300 and recall 32/50, with Wilson 95% intervals from the historical precision audit. Crosses show frozen requirements 0.90 for accuracy and 0.80 for advancement recall. Both benchmark versions failed their original primary acceptance rules. Macro-F1 and other class recalls remain in the full metric JSONs and are not hidden by this focused exhibit. Synthetic projects are not biological ground truth or a representative literature sample; Wilson intervals here describe a binomial-style precision summary conditional on the toy benchmark, not external generalization. The old plot title's word “Independent” refers to computational recomputation, not independent human review. No new simulation or threshold tuning was performed.

H1/H2 are NOT Figures 2/3 of the current prospective R1 manuscript. A separate bounded negative-results note would require its own scope, complete methods, locked-case chronology and explicit author decision. Do not reuse published reference-paper subpanels as local biological evidence.
