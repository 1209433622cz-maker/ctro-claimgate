# Scientific spine and section architecture — supervisory decision

## 1. What manuscript does the evidence support today?

The local workspace is scientifically rich in failure audits, source reconciliation, code provenance and prospective design, but not in R1 empirical observations. Those are different forms of value. A file-count, intact ZIP or software agreement cannot be a substitute for measured claim–evidence alignment.

The defensible current route is an externally reviewable **prospective meta-research design**, with the future original empirical article conditional on an approved frame, measurement instrument and genuine independent ratings. This is not a literature review. It also is not the disease-axis discovery paper originally sought: none of the frozen disease projects completed its prespecified advancement chain. A second, bounded negative-results methods note is possible in principle, but must be explicitly chosen and must not silently replace R1.

Recommended current title:

> Claim–evidence alignment in chronic-disease single-cell transcriptomics: a prospective meta-research design

The former leading word “Mechanistic” excluded adequate descriptive cell-state claims from the apparent scope, while the protocol considers both descriptive disease-associated cell-state claims and inferred communication. The corrected title aligns with the proposed unit without suggesting a population estimate has already been obtained.

## 2. One question, five inferential links

Question: **For one claim chosen by an approved rule per eligible paper version in a defined bibliographic frame, what public evidence supports the proposition, and what distribution can validly be estimated?**

| Link | Necessary observation or rule | Consequence if missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population → sampled papers | Approved frame, coverage, version, probabilities including pilot adaptation | Random-looking selection is not enough for generalization |
| Paper → one fixed claim | Original qualifiers and objective-linked or probabilistic tie rule frozen before rating | Selecting the weakest claim makes the endpoint outcome-dependent |
| Claim → biological evidence | Donor/specimen/library/cell hierarchy, assay target, model, overlap | Cells or repeated biopsies cannot automatically become independent people |
| Evidence → reproducible state | Content-valid nominal definitions, precedence, independent human coding | A four-label form is not yet a validated measurement instrument |
| Observed states → inferential denominator | Eligibility and claim-fixation states, unresolved mass, approved estimator and uncertainty | Complete-record ratio does not automatically identify all eligible papers |

This chain, not an eight-figure quota or a collection of attractive single-cell panels, must drive the manuscript.

## 3. Section logic and ownership

| Section | Question answered | Evidence owner | Prohibited shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction, paragraph 1 | What is already known about interpretation? | Verified conceptual and analytic literature | Claiming to invent observation–inference–claim distinction |
| Introduction, paragraphs 2–4 | Why do units and assay targets matter for a specific proposition? | Replicate and communication-method papers | Assuming every published claim is invalid |
| Introduction, final paragraph | What remains empirical rather than conceptual? | Defined distribution and traceable sampling target | “Mismatch is common” without population data |
| Objectives | Exactly which claim and paper denominator are targeted? | Paper-level index-claim rule | Generalizing to all claims, all single-cell research or all chronic diseases |
| Methods | How would the inferential links be implemented? | Scope, search, sampling, fixed claim, units, states, independent coding, estimator, selected C5 | Past tense or registration assertions before execution |
| Current evidence/status | Which empirical quantities do not yet exist? | Recorded gate and staffing status | Filling results with historical selected disease cases |
| Discussion | What would the study add, and under what assumptions? | Question-specific interpretation, missingness and decision alternatives | Claiming software integrity demonstrates scientific validity |
| Limitations/conclusion | What can and cannot be concluded now? | Unsettled approvals, no formal sample, no content-validity evidence | A perfect-sounding final mechanistic claim |

## 4. Historical evidence: important, but in the right manuscript

SSc demonstrates cross-scale and model dependence, IgAN discovery–replication reversal, and Endometriosis sensitivity to a highly collinear conditional model. PBC and corrected LN demonstrate that fixed discovery criteria were not met in small discovery cohorts. HFpEF demonstrates lack of a claim-compatible measurement/independent validation architecture before any expression test. Together they support a record of disciplined development decisions, **not six proven absent disease mechanisms**.

ORGAXIS V1/V2 demonstrate that deterministic replay and independently recomputed predictions can coexist with failure to meet frozen performance requirements. They do not demonstrate reliable classification of a real literature population. Six selected internal cases and 300 balanced toy projects have different sampling targets and should not be pooled.

The supplementary H1/H2 exhibits preserve these lessons for author discussion. Main R1 Results must come from the authorized external corpus, not these exhibits.
