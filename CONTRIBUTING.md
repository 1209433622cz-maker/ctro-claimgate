# Contributing

Contributions should protect the prospective boundary of the study.

Before proposing a change:

1. identify the affected RP v4 gate and any retained RP v3 lock;
2. state whether the change was conceived before or after outcome access;
3. preserve immutable source wording and distinguish corrections from reinterpretations;
4. do not upload expression matrices, cell-level metadata, third-party full text, author repositories, reviewer packets, credentials, or local receipts;
5. add or update a deterministic validation check where possible;
6. do not change frozen thresholds to rescue an observed result;
7. never present an internal agent, template, or unsigned form as an independent human review.

Issues and pull requests may discuss public methods, schemas, reproducibility, and documentation. They must not disclose confidential author correspondence, reviewer identities, personal data, or restricted source material.
